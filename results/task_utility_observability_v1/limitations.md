# Scientific scope and threats

This is an exploratory reanalysis designed AFTER the Experiment21 development results were known. Freezing this analysis before fitting its probes is useful reproducibility discipline, not preregistration of an independent discovery. UCF101 official-test scientific outputs are prohibited inputs and are not a replacement validation population.

The existing recognition head fitted original training clips, including all current probe-fitting groups, and selected regularization using ALL original development clips. Original controller selection and execution development outcomes were already exposed. The head also reached its recorded optimizer iteration limit. Neither head nor original controllers are refitted. The current train/selection/execution group separation is real but does not remove prior head-selection exposure or make head/controller fitting independent.

Groups are UCF filename (class,gXX) units with exact/known duplicate-component checks. Unknown parent sources, reuploads and checkpoint-pretraining mappings remain unresolved. Different gXX labels or a dataset name are not proof of universal source/pretraining independence. The protocol includes only the technically eligible inherited two-clips-per-group population, not every original UCF video or the official three-split benchmark.

The primary periodic prefix distribution avoids outcome-based state selection. It is still a conditional observational distribution. Secondary random and H+M trajectories share the SAME clips and are not independent replications. The H+M policy itself was selected and exposed previously. Transporting fixed periodic-fitted probes to H+M states measures distribution sensitivity, not policy value or optimality.

Target A evaluates the most recent cache feature versus the current genuine feature, not final pooling. Target B changes the current branch while optimizing every feasible future completion with labels and future embeddings. It is a privileged continuation bound, not an online measurement or downstream treatment effect of deploying another policy. Positive/zero/near-zero gains must be considered alongside magnitudes; high sign AUC on negligible gains would not establish useful recognition improvement.

No labels, current uncomputed X3D features, future thumbnails/features, teachers or target losses enter the causal vector. H includes the full cached head probability vector, which may expose class structure through genuine past observations. Predictability on the same101 classes is not evidence of transfer to unseen classes or datasets. Constant/linear probes are deliberately limited: a negative result cannot prove information-theoretic absence, and a positive result does not prove that the old learner could exploit the signal with its different proxy-imitation target.

Bootstrap draws carry whole groups, clips, states and paired methods together. They condition on fitted/selected/calibrated models, the reused head and inherited population. They omit all fitting/selection/exposure uncertainty, unresolved parent-source relationships and new-dataset shift. One/two execution groups per class are particularly limiting; one-group strata contribute no estimated within-class variance. Selection has one group/class. All intervals are marginal/unadjusted exploratory summaries; no equivalence or power claim follows. Strata are descriptive conditional populations with explicit class/group coverage, not new discoveries.

Costs are genuine previous fresh-decode GPU executions, not CPU replay estimates. Per-video medians use five measured trials after two warmups; repeats are not sources. The30 timing groups do not represent all101 classes. The matched30 quality/cost frontier is distinguished from the400-quality/30-cost combination. Both are descriptive point comparisons with measurement variation, not proof of an optimized deployment frontier. Thumbnail/statistics times are nested inside observation preparation. Full-clip timestamp planning, common causal trace bookkeeping, CPU readback, per-forward synchronization and native400 projection remain in the measured boundary. No fused decoder, optimized thumbnail path, new streaming implementation or hypothetical speedup has been measured here.

The two retained execution interruptions concern file persistence and resume authentication, before probe fitting/prediction. Both original code/seals and errors are archived. The amendments change no target, population, feature, probe, selection or statistical rule; they are disclosed rather than hidden. Passing unit tests is distinct from completing genuine target replay, probe analysis and independent evidence verification.

A post-initial-analysis reporting correction makes constant-prediction Spearman undefined rather than reporting tiny numerical residuals. The complete initial analysis is archived locally with a public hash inventory; parameters, calibrators, thresholds and all prediction vectors are verified bitwise unchanged after the fixed CPU refit/reanalysis. A separate exact-bit standardization validation was overly strict: different BLAS output widths yielded only1.63e-14 absolute moment differences. The original check failure is retained; the disclosed1e-12 tolerance passes without altering observations, normalization rules or fitted models.
