# Competing explanations: exploratory evidence, not unique causal attribution

|Explanation|Evidence and status|What remains unresolved|
|---|---|---|
|H1: fidelity teacher insufficiently task aligned|**Direct mismatch observed; supported descriptively.** On identical periodic prefixes, teacher/task agreement is51.11% for immediate gain and56.60% for budget gain;14.27% of weighted states request teacher refresh while budget gain is negative. Exact true-label oracle headroom0.111945 nats greatly exceeds Z_pool's0.001576 reduction.|No claim that every feature-fidelity objective fails, or that teacher disagreement alone causally explains final controller loss.|
|H2: causal observations insufficient|**Consistent for the incremental M pathway in these probes.** H+M-H AP is+0.000181 for local and-0.001462 for budget; M-only is near base rate, secondary populations show no robust incremental pattern. History alone does carry modest local sign information.|Linear probes cannot establish information-theoretic absence, failure of all causal features, or transfer to another dataset. The budget target deliberately contains unpredictable future/label information.|
|H3: information exists but learner fails|**Unresolved.** H improves immediate-sign classification over structural features, but M does not demonstrate useful added information; local gain magnitude regression is worse than the constant predictor. Existing neural policies learn proxy imitation rather than task gain.|Different objectives, nonlinear capacity, head-training overlap and large target-scale shift confound attributing failure uniquely to the learner. Do not add architectures to seek a positive result.|
|H4: overhead removes advantage|**Cost directly observed.** Paired median H+M overhead152.2ms over periodic; unused gate135.3ms. H+M is dominated by periodic on the matched30 quality/cost cohort.|The claim that decisions were useful until overhead removed their advantage is not supported: recognition improvement was not established. A hypothetical optimized decoder is unmeasured.|
|H5: too little offline headroom|**Blanket task-headroom explanation not supported by this cohort.** Exact true-label hindsight reduces periodic CE0.247427 to0.135483; conditional group interval for headroom[0.089473,0.133653]. Fidelity-proxy headroom is small/inconclusive.|The large label/future-informed bound may be unobservable or unreachable by causal B4 decisions. It is not a guaranteed deployable opportunity.|

Evidence values and conditional group intervals are calculated from genuine retained inputs; see findings.md and alignment_headroom_group_intervals.json. The hold-label comparison is a frequency statistic, not a newly executed always-hold policy. Local/branch regret is retrospective attribution under a fixed prefix, not a downstream policy treatment effect. Multiple explanations remain compatible.
